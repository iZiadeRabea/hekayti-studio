# Hekayti Paper-Cut Style Bible
D style profile hek-paper-cut/0.1. Practical direction, not final art approved by owner. Core: COLORFUL LAYERED PAPER-CUT CHILDREN'S ILLUSTRATION.

## Materials and shapes

Rounded asymmetric cut-paper silhouettes, slightly imperfect tidy edges; visible layered coloured cardstock and subtle fibres at print scale. Paper texture supports shape, never noisy photo grain. Build foreground/midground/background layers with shallow soft shadow separation. One diffuse upper-left light cue throughout scene; no glossy/plastic specular, extreme depth-of-field or photorealistic material.

Faces: simple wide readable shapes, small oval/dot eyes with consistent spacing; restrained pupil/highlight grammar; small curved/oval mouth, clear expression by brow/posture. No glass eyeballs, porcelain skin or uncanny human detail. Toddlers rounded head/body; older characters slightly less exaggerated. Exact proportions locked in bible, not random per shot.

Hands: stylized soft mittens or carefully defined fingers as locked for character; limbs connected, one obvious action; intended anatomy/assistive devices respected. Clothing uses cut shapes with minimal seam/pattern cues, base colours and accessories locked. Animals use paper silhouettes with species-consistent appendages.

## World and colour

Environments: rounded buildings/interiors, broad furniture shapes; layered leaves/trunks, a few readable plant silhouettes. Egyptian everyday settings without tourism-only cliches, poverty stereotypes or obligatory religious markers. Owner reviews cultural/faith specifics. Do not insert text on signs without separate approved typography.

Provisional palette: warm cream #FFF4DE, coral #EF7564, golden yellow #F5C85B, teal #3C9D96, sky blue #73B7D8, leaf green #7CAE71; dark ink #28333D. Store palette roles, not “six colours everywhere.” Consistent character colour IDs; scene lighting may vary slightly. Colour never sole information cue; dark/light contrast and shapes remain readable. Test printed proof; hex RGB is not a press-colour guarantee.

## Composition / complexity by age (D)

Under1: one big recognisable subject on separated ground, warm/simple or high-contrast variant, few layers.
1–2: one action and few large props, stable environment.
2–3: one clear result/change with limited background anchors.
3–5: foreground action plus supportive location/clue, no competing mini-stories.
5–8: some secondary details if hierarchy remains clear, never “find 20 tiny things” by default.

Page aspect ratio follows output profile, not provider default. Reserve a declared quiet text rectangle at top/bottom/side; child face/action and crucial prop outside it. For Arabic align block logically RTL; don't mirror character identity or directional event just to move text.

## Prompt recipe / prohibitions

Scene intent + exact CharacterBible appearance + state/prop anchors + camera/action/emotion + paper-cut material/lighting/palette + output ratio/quiet text area + mustShow/mustNotShow.
Use illustrated reference IDs/hash and approved page states; never just “same child.”

Negative direction: no photorealism, glossy plastic 3D, uncanny face, extra unintended limbs, clutter, illegible embedded Arabic, unrelated photo textures, style changes, copyrighted characters or imitation of named living artists. All book text including title composed deterministically later, even if a skill allows model-drawn cover text.

No art generated. First later visual pilot: owner approves reference sheet, cover concept and one interior before bulk work. Record approved style version; apply changes intentionally and recheck dependencies.
