# Egyptian Arabic
D: house style ar-eg/0.1. Natural written Egyptian narration, reviewed by an Egyptian children's editor; not a word-for-word MSA conversion. No official universal Egyptian orthography claimed.

## Working register / glossary

Friendly broadly understandable urban Egyptian, avoiding region/class caricature. Prefer everyday forms such as هنا، كده، تعال، بصّ، يلا، واحدة واحدة، هنجرّب تاني when natural in the situation. A polite invitation may be more appropriate than an imperative. Maintain a project glossary: approved spelling, pronunciation, MSA meaning and permitted variants.

Provisional choices: عايز/عايزة، مش، دلوقتي، إزاي، دي/ده; use consistently within edition, including negation and attached forms. Owner/editor can revise glossary version; do not mix spellings arbitrarily. No Arabizi/Latin digits replacing Arabic letters, profanity, adult memes, harsh taunts or excessive slang. Do not equate dialect with grammatical carelessness.

## Age and readability

Familiar concrete actions and shorter turns; complexity follows Age Engine but dialect contractions are not automatically easier. Avoid idioms that need adult background. Caregiver prompts outside story text. Keep child-friendly sound play only if intelligible and culturally appropriate; don't force a rhyme from MSA.

Narration and dialogue share the chosen mode; names/character mannerisms stay stable. Limited mixed words permitted only by explicit glossary. Keep dignity, realistic emotion and supportive adult presence. No mockery of accents, bodies or “bad children.”

Use Arabic punctuation and RTL handling from arabic-guidelines.md. Diacritics selective for ambiguity/pronunciation; fully diacritized Egyptian edition requires an agreed spelling convention first. Pronunciation script should be approved by an Egyptian speaker; never infer speech from MSA vocalization.

## Approval / regression

Native read-aloud across complete book, then compare beat/page IDs, object quantities, emotions and educational intent to canonical meaning. Typography/captions checked separately. An MSA-to-dialect rewrite does not inherit source language approval. Keep corrected terms in glossary and invalidate narration derived from changed prose.
