## Research method / unanswered questions

Questions: what capabilities are documented; how does shared reading differ from independent literacy; what changes across precise bands; what is known about screens; which local architecture minimizes rewrite?

Method: current official pages retrieved with web tools; concise paraphrases, no bulk copied articles. Evidence confidence high for a source's stated guidance, only medium for transfer to Egyptian publishing. Editorial ranges and outcomes remain unvalidated.

Dead end: older AAP TV-viewing URL returned 404. Searched current AAP policy instead; do not repeat old 2016 rules as latest 2026 policy. No unsupported universal age-times-attention formula adopted. No randomized effectiveness evidence for these specific products, Arabic vocabulary quotas or image-clutter counts found in this review.

Recheck sources before release, when a guideline changes, or at the next substantive age-rule update. Record changed claims, date, reviewer and affected AgeProfile version. Do not silently change released stories. Qualified early-childhood reviewer, native Egyptian editor and privacy/legal reviewer remain unassigned; web research is not their approval.
