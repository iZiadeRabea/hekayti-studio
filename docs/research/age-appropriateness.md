# Age appropriateness — design translation
All rules marked D below are editorial choices informed by child-development.md, not clinically validated prescriptions.

## Capability-to-content mapping

| Band | Language / structure (D) | Visual / emotional / interaction direction (D) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–11 months | Label, sound, repeated tiny phrase; sequence not plot | One clear focal subject; warm expression; adult points/responds; stop on disengagement |
| 12–23 months | Familiar noun + action, tiny routine | Large recognisable objects, one action; adult-assisted success, pointing optional |
| 24–35 months | A small causal episode, two linked actions introduced gradually | Simple pretend scenario, visible result, supported feelings; limited choices |
| 36–59 months | Setup → attempts → choice → warm outcome | A few character relationships; simple prediction; manageable worry and repair |
| 60–95 months | More linked events, literal plus modest inference | Limited subproblem, named mixed feelings; caregiver or emerging reader mode selected separately |

Sources underpinning the progression: [CDC 18m](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/18-months.html), [30m](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/30-months.html), [4y](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/4-years.html), [5y](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/5-years.html). They do not establish the exact Hekayti ranges.

## Screens and product eligibility

E: WHO's 2019 guidance recommends no sedentary screens for under-two children; ages two to four have a daily upper recommendation of one hour, less preferred. [WHO official summary](https://www.who.int/news-room/detail/24-04-2019-to-grow-up-healthy-children-need-to-sit-less-and-play-more).
E: The newer 2026 AAP guidance emphasizes context, child-centered design and caregiver communication, replacing prior AAP media guidance. [AAP 2026](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/news/Pages/creating-a-child-friendly-digital-world-AAP-releases-new-media-recommendations.aspx).
D: Do not claim AAP and WHO are identical. Hekayti adopts conservative under-two child-viewing exclusion; print/adult-read book primary. A parent-facing digital file is not an instruction to place a baby in front of a screen. Under-two child video delivery is disabled by default; parent-only production preview must be labelled. For eligible older stories use co-viewing, pause/stop controls, finite endings, no autoplay, ads, addictive loops or bedtime-screen promotion. A short video is not automatically safe or educational.

## No false precision

No universal minutes-per-year attention law, comprehension score, fixed vocabulary ceiling, mandatory page count or cutoff for all adventure. Numeric budgets in age-engine.md are D/H; native-language read-aloud and caregiver response govern edits. Arabic word count is not interchangeable with English length. Never infer a child's intelligence, diagnosis or treatment from age settings.

Safety across bands: no humiliation, fear-based compliance, corporal punishment, dangerous imitation, sexualization, stereotypes or guaranteed therapy. Do not use an educational storyline as instructions for risky medical/physical procedures. Accessibility options may simplify wording or artwork at any age without labels.

Validate with voluntary guardian-supervised feedback and qualified review before production; do not collect child metrics unnecessarily. Record scope, language, profile version and any justified override. No participant testing performed in Phase 1.
