# Extensible patterns and genuine feedback

Patterns use the full structural vector: goal + conflict + mechanism + attempt sequence + turn + resolution. Names, themes and settings do not define identity. Canonical signatures/IDs recompute on validation; compact IDs are indexing labels, not cryptographic proof or official tale-type classification. Group by complete signature; candidate pattern-ID collisions must not be taken as evidence of sameness.

Lion/Mouse, Ant/Dove and Peasant/Eagle share one reciprocal-help family despite different casts; Wolf/Crane's denied reward has a different structure despite a related help theme. Similarity is allowed. The records stay GENERATED, not automatically approved examples. Existing explicit lifecycle/family/variant actions remain the authority for operational promotion and use.

patternFeedback aggregates actual persisted USED/APPROVED/REJECTED outcomes, variant relationships and SCORE event ratings/notes. Imported/seeded scores and historical usage summaries do not become current analytics. Averages include a count, not fabricated seeded reviews. Parent/native/age/language/story/reuse opinions must be actual owner actions; source extraction provides no fake feedback.
