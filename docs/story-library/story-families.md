# Structural story families and variants

A STORY_FAMILY stores a narrative pattern, mechanism, conflict pattern, resolution pattern, emotional arc and reusable constraints—not a complete story copied into every book. It can be created explicitly from an idea or approved reference with human-authored fields. Generation uses a promoted, versioned family snapshot as data, never instruction authority.

A variant links to an exact family revision and has both a stable variantId and positive variantNumber. Variant identities and numbers are unique within the current library; revision history retains older links. A variant link is assigned once, not silently retargeted. Family detail lists related variants and usage. Family structural context survives later archiving through immutable package/history snapshots; archived/current-unpromoted records cannot be newly selected as curated context.

SimilarVersion intentionally retains structure closely. NewVariant retains family identity while changing creative surfaces. FreshConcept uses blocks/context as inspiration without retaining family identity. CompletelyNew has no creative obligation to follow selected context. Similarity is allowed, not automatically treated as a duplicate defect. Imported candidates still require explicit human linkage/promotion; an AI response cannot create arbitrary family relations or approvals.
